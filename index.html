Privacy Policy

Your work belongs to you. This policy explains how DigiBoard handles information when you use the app, including local projects, recordings, advertising and subscription services.

Please read this policy carefully to understand the app's data practices.
At a glance: DigiBoard is designed primarily for on-device teaching and creation. The app does not operate a developer-owned account system or cloud backend. 1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the DigiBoard Android application and describes how information is handled when you use its teaching, whiteboard, presentation, recording, export, storage and related features.

DigiBoard is intended for users of different age groups. The app provides an age-group selection and applies protective advertising settings based on that selection. The app does not ask users to enter their date of birth as part of this selection.

2. Information the app handles

Depending on the features you choose to use, DigiBoard may process or store:

  • Projects, boards, slides, drawings, notes, templates and related thumbnails.
  • Imported documents and images, and files created through export features.
  • Audio recordings, screen recordings and optional camera video created after you start those features.
  • App preferences, selected age group, local security settings and locally stored subscription or reward state.
  • Information needed by Google Play Billing, Google Mobile Ads and applicable consent services when those services are used.

DigiBoard does not ask for a name, phone number, email address or date of birth to create an in-app user account. The app does not provide a developer-operated account or cloud-sync backend.

3. Information stored on your device

Projects and app data are stored locally using app-private files, local databases and device preferences. Depending on your activity, local storage may include project data, drafts, thumbnails, imported files, recordings, templates, diagram assets, app settings and security-related values.

Some security values are stored as derived hashes rather than as the original PIN or recovery answer. Local storage is used to support app features and does not mean that the information is uploaded to a DigiBoard server.

4. Accounts and developer backend

DigiBoard does not provide user registration, user login, a developer-operated cloud database or a developer-operated file storage service. Your teaching projects and recordings are designed to remain on your device unless you choose to export or share them, or a third-party Android or Google service handles information as described in this policy.

5. Age groups and safety settings

On first use, DigiBoard asks the user to select an age group: Under 13, 13–17, or 18+. The selected group is stored locally so that the app can apply the appropriate experience and advertising safeguards. The app does not request an exact date of birth for this purpose.

If the age group is missing, invalid or unknown, the app uses protective settings and blocks advertising until the age state is resolved. For users selected as Under 13 or 13–17, the app applies child-directed or under-age advertising safeguards as configured in the app.

The in-app arithmetic parental gate is an adult-presence check for certain protected actions. It is not identity verification and should not be understood as a standalone parental identity-verification or verifiable parental-consent service.

6. Advertising

The free version of DigiBoard may display ads provided by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Advertising may involve information processed by Google and its advertising partners, such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address, app interaction signals and other information described in Google's own documentation and settings.

DigiBoard applies age-based ad request settings. For Under 13, 13–17 and unknown age groups, the app applies protective settings intended to request child-safe, non-personalized advertising, including applicable child-directed and under-age treatment flags and a general-audience content rating. The exact ad availability also depends on supported inventory and provider rules.

For users selected as 18+, advertising requests are subject to the applicable consent process and regional requirements. Pro users are not shown DigiBoard ads. The app's optional rewarded-ad feature may offer a temporary Smart Pen unlock after the required completed rewarded-ad views.

Google's handling of information for advertising is governed by Google's policies. See Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob Help.

7. Consent and privacy choices

For eligible 18+ free users in regions where consent is required, DigiBoard uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage applicable advertising consent choices. Ads are gated according to the consent status returned by the consent process.

Under-age and unknown-age users follow the app's protective age-based flow rather than the adult UMP flow. Pro users do not receive ads and do not need the advertising consent flow for DigiBoard ad requests. Where Google provides a privacy-options form for an eligible user, the app may make that choice available through its settings.

Consent requirements and available choices can vary by region and by Google's services.

8. Subscriptions and payments

DigiBoard uses Google Play Billing for Pro subscription purchases and related subscription operations. Google Play handles payment details and provides purchase information to the app so it can determine the available Pro entitlement.

DigiBoard does not receive or store your full payment-card details. Google Play may process account, transaction, purchase-token and subscription information under Google's terms and privacy policy. Subscription availability, renewal, cancellation, refunds and payment methods are subject to Google Play's processes.

Some subscription state is cached locally to support entitlement checks. A locally stored state is not a substitute for Google's authoritative purchase records.

See Google Payments and Google Play Help for more information.

9. Device permissions

DigiBoard requests device permissions when needed for a feature you choose to use. Depending on your Android version and actions, these may include microphone, camera, notifications and access through Android's file picker, media or document interfaces.

The app does not need access to your contacts or precise location for its core whiteboard and teaching functions. Android may display separate system permission prompts or file-selection screens. You can manage app permissions in Android Settings; denying a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

10. Camera and microphone

The microphone is used when you explicitly start a voice or screen-recording feature that includes audio. The camera is used when you explicitly enable the optional Face Camera feature. These features create recordings for your use and save them to the device or a location you select.

DigiBoard does not claim to record camera or microphone input continuously in the background. Recording behaviour is subject to Android permissions, foreground-service requirements and the controls shown in the app and operating system.

11. On-device recognition and processing

Text recognition (OCR) features use on-device processing where supported by the integrated ML Kit functionality. The app uses this processing to provide the requested feature. Availability and model behaviour may depend on the device and installed component versions.

12. User-provided image URLs

If you choose a feature that fetches an image from a URL you provide, DigiBoard makes a network request to that address to retrieve the image. The app applies a file-size limit to this fetch operation. The destination website or server may receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, and handles it under its own privacy policy.

Only enter URLs from sources you trust. Network access for this feature is separate from the app's local whiteboard and drawing functions.

13. Backup and restore

DigiBoard's Android backup configuration excludes selected sensitive preferences and app data, including age-selection and security-related preference stores, subscription and reward state, consent preferences, security backup material and specified recording-related folders. Other app-private project files and the local database may be included in Android backup or device-transfer operations according to Android's backup behaviour and your device settings.

When app data is restored, the app validates the stored age state and uses a protective unknown-age state if a valid selection is not available. Backup availability and behaviour may vary by Android version, device manufacturer and user settings.

14. Exports, sharing and printing

When you export, print or share a project, image, PDF, presentation or recording, the app uses Android storage, document, media, print or sharing facilities as appropriate. Files saved to shared device locations, such as DigiBoard folders in Documents, Movies or Pictures, are outside the app's private storage.

Shared or exported files may remain on your device after DigiBoard is uninstalled. If you send a file to another app, person or service, that recipient's own terms and privacy practices apply.

15. Data retention and deletion

App-private projects, drafts, recordings and settings generally remain on the device until you delete them, clear app data or uninstall the app, subject to Android backup and restore behaviour. Files exported to shared storage may need to be deleted separately using your device's file or gallery app.

Clearing a Recent Files or Recent Recordings list may remove an item from the app's recent-history display without deleting the underlying file. Use the relevant delete action or your device's file manager to remove the actual file.

DigiBoard does not maintain a developer-operated cloud account from which it can retrieve or delete your local projects.

16. Third-party services

The app integrates with or relies on third-party Android and Google services, which may include:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for advertising.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for applicable consent and privacy choices.
  • Google Play Billing for Pro subscriptions and purchases.
  • Google Play services for supported in-app update and platform functions.
  • Google ML Kit on-device text recognition functionality.
  • Android system services for camera, microphone, storage, media, printing and file sharing.

These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms. DigiBoard does not control the independent data practices of third-party providers.

17. Security

DigiBoard uses Android app-private storage and local security mechanisms for supported data. Local security features include salted password-based hashing for PIN and recovery-related values. No method of storage, transmission or software security can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should keep your device operating system updated, use an appropriate device lock and avoid sharing exported files with untrusted recipients.

18. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when app features, integrated services, legal requirements or data practices change. The current version will be published at the location where this policy is made available. Please review this page periodically.

If a future app update introduces a new way of collecting, sharing or processing information, the policy should be updated to describe that change before or when the feature is released, as applicable.
